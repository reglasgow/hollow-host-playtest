Future updates stay in the same app.

Enable Auto-update on the game’s Play Store page. Approved releases arrive through Google Play; you can also tap Update there. Availability may take time after publication. You do not need a new invitation or APK for each update.

Keep the app installed to retain local progress. An older APK installed outside Google Play is a separate app: its saves do not transfer automatically.

Google Play says “App not available”?

Check that you joined the group in step 1 and opened both pages with the Google account used in the phone’s Play Store. Then reopen step 2. A different account cannot see the closed test.

If you previously joined the developer’s internal test, you may need to leave that test before joining this closed test. You can keep receiving updates through the internal test instead if you are still eligible.

Use an Android device for installation. An iPhone cannot install this Android game. For help, leave a message on the game page.
